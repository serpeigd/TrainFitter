# Nutrient Absorption Synergies

> A hallmark of the method: not just *what* to eat, but *how to combine it* to
> absorb it better. Especially relevant for vegetarian/vegan diets.

## Combinations that boost absorption
| Target nutrient | Booster | Practical example |
|---|---|---|
| Beta-carotene / Vit. A | Fat (oil, avocado) | Carrot/tomato + olive oil |
| Vitamins D, E, K | Dietary fat | Vitamin D supplement with a fatty meal (never on an empty stomach) |
| Non-heme iron (plant) | Vitamin C (50-150 mg) | Lentils/spinach + lemon, red pepper, or kiwi |
| Curcumin | Piperine (black pepper) + fat | Turmeric + a pinch of pepper + oil (↑ up to 20×) |
| Zinc / heme iron | Animal protein | Legumes + egg or meat |
| Probiotics | Prebiotic fiber | Kefir/yogurt + oats or slightly underripe banana |

## Interference to avoid
- **Iron inhibitors:** tea, coffee, cocoa (tannins) and calcium (dairy) reduce
  non-heme iron absorption → **separate by 1-2 h** from iron-rich meals.
- **Phytates** (unsoaked grains/legumes): chelate minerals. Soaking 8-12 h,
  sprouting, or fermenting reduces them.

## Preparation that improves bioavailability
- Soak legumes and discard the water; sprout seeds; ferment (sourdough, miso).
- Garlic: chop and let it sit for 10 min before cooking (activates allicin).
- Briefly steam cruciferous vegetables (preserves sulforaphane better than boiling).

## Timing across the day
- Vitamin D and omega-3s with the day's fattiest meal.
- Plant iron + vitamin C at lunch/dinner, away from tea/coffee/dairy.
- Magnesium/zinc at night, separated from calcium or iron.

> **Clinical note:** always flag that this doesn't replace a healthcare
> professional's assessment, especially with medication that interacts with iron,
> calcium, or vitamin K (e.g. blood thinners).
